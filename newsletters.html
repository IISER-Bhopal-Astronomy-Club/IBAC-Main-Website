Latest Issue Summer 2025

Starsight Summer '25

After a long wait, we are pleased to bring you our newest issue of Starsight for Summer '25, delving into interstellar voyagers, cosmic genesis, and the existential timeline of our universe.

Featured Topics
  • 3I/ATLAS (C/2025 N1): An introduction to the third known interstellar comet and what it reveals about planetary formation beyond our solar system.
  • Origin of our Solar System: From swirling clouds of primordial dust to the cosmic forces that molded planets.
  • Astrobiology & Rare Earth: An exploration into the ingredients that constitute life and why intelligent life is extraordinarily rare.
  • Fate of the Universe: From uncanny scenarios to heat death and the Big Rip.
  • Regular Features: Sky News, fresh puzzle in the Game section, and our special Astro-Calendar!

Seasonal Edition Summer 2024

Starsight Summer '24

Following tremendous community support, the Summer '24 edition documents May, June, and July activities, recapping insightful talks and student research breakthroughs.

Featured Topics
  • Summer Talk Summaries: Comprehensive recaps of astrophysical seminars and classroom talks conducted over the summer.
  • Student Research Abstracts: Abstracts and direct pointers to latest research articles written by IBAC core members.
  • Sky News Digest: Highlights of rare astronomical events and observation notes from the campus dome observatory.
  • Did You Know? Section: Astonishing cosmic facts and curiosities to broaden your scientific horizons.
  • Game & Puzzle Section: Brand new astronomical challenge and crosswords for space enthusiasts.

Autumn Issue Sep – Oct 2023

Starsight Sep-Oct '23

The platform for budding stargazers and space enthusiasts to delve even deeper into the mysteries of the cosmos and stay attuned to campus astronomical initiatives (think IBAC Pro!).

Featured Topics
  • Condensed Talk Digests: Quick-read summaries of guest lectures for students caught in lectures and quizzes.
  • Livestream Reports: Special coverages of the Aditya-L1 launch and Chandrayaan-3 lunar landing screenings on campus.
  • Forthcoming Astronomical Events: Calendar of conjunctions, meteor showers, and sky alignments.
  • Fun Trivia & Games: Teasers of intriguing articles, astrophysics trivia, and skygazing challenges.

Inaugural Issue August 2023

Starsight August '23

The inaugural edition of Starsight that ignited IBAC's newsletter tradition! Featuring introductory essays, observatory equipment features, and community spotlights.

Featured Topics
  • The Launch of Starsight: Establishing an open astronomical dialogue across IISER Bhopal.
  • Observatory Showcase: Insights into our 14-inch Cassegrain telescope and Dobsonian "Dobby".
  • Planetary Viewing Guides: Beginner tutorials on observing Saturn's rings and Jupiter's Galilean moons.
  • Student Astrophotography: The inaugural portfolio of lunar craters and stellar clusters.
