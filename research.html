About The Group

The Student Research Wing (IBARG)

Bridging foundational undergraduate coursework with professional astrophysical research methodologies.

Genesis & Scientific Mission

Founded in December 2018 by Parth Nayak, IBARG is the student-led research wing of the IISER Bhopal Astronomy Club. We maintain privileged operational access to IISER Bhopal's rooftop observatory and work closely with faculty mentors from the Department of Physics on high-impact astrophysical questions.

Our mission is two-fold: to train students in the rigorous methodologies of observational data acquisition, CCD reduction, and telescope mount operation, while cultivating active research projects spanning pulsar timing, AGN spectra, exoplanetary characterization, and computational cosmological simulations.

Today, IBARG alumni and members hold research fellowships, author peer-reviewed manuscripts, and pursue doctoral and postdoctoral studies at top institutions worldwide, including Columbia University, Adam Mickiewicz University, IUCAA, NCRA-TIFR, and the Max Planck Institutes.

Infrastructure & Instruments

Observation Facilities

State-of-the-art optical and tracking hardware housed atop Academic Building 1 (AB-1) on the IISER Bhopal campus.

AB-1 Observatory Dome

Perched atop Academic Building 1 (AB-1), our fully rotating dome provides unobstructed 360-degree viewing of the night sky over the Bhopal campus, isolating precision instruments from local ambient light and campus wind currents.

14-inch Cassegrain Telescope

A research-grade 14" Cassegrain optical system designed for high-resolution planetary imaging, deep-sky astrophotography, variable star time-series photometry, and narrowband spectroscopic follow-ups.

German Equatorial Mount (GEM)

Heavy-duty motorized computer-controlled equatorial tracking mount ensuring sub-arcsecond tracking precision and extended exposure stability necessary for faint deep-sky targets and transit light curves.

Cooled CCD & Filter Wheel

Equipped with thermoelectrically cooled astronomical CCD cameras and standardized Johnson-Cousins (UBVRI) photometric filters, enabling calibrated stellar magnitude determination and colour-magnitude diagram synthesis.

Astrophysical Data Pipeline

Dedicated computational workflows utilizing AstroPy, IRAF, SAOImage DS9, and custom Python pipelines for dark subtraction, flat fielding, aperture photometry, and cosmic ray removal.

Multi-Wavelength Archival Access

Student researchers routinely cross-match on-campus optical observations with multi-wavelength space and ground surveys, including Chandra, XMM-Newton, Gaia, SDSS, JWST, and GMRT data archives.

Scientific Frontier

Key Research Themes & Domains

Core domains explored by IBARG student researchers under the guidance of IISER Bhopal faculty and international collaborators.

High-Energy Astrophysics & Relativistic Jets

Investigating accretion disc dynamics around stellar-mass and supermassive black holes, relativistic jet launching mechanisms, high-energy X-ray flaring episodes, and multimessenger signals from compact binary mergers.

Pulsar Timing & Gravitational Waves

Studying nanoHertz gravitational wave backgrounds and compact star physics utilizing Indian Pulsar Timing Array (InPTA) and International Pulsar Timing Array (IPTA) datasets and pulse dispersion delay analysis.

Quasars & Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN)

Computational analysis, SED fitting, and spectral decomposition of active galactic nuclei to understand early galaxy co-evolution, supermassive black hole seed formation, and broad emission line kinematics.

Star Clusters & Stellar Kinematics

Analyzing embedded clusters in molecular clouds, blue straggler populations, stellar velocity dispersions, mass segregation, and the dynamical evolution of Galactic open and globular clusters.

Exoplanetary Habitability & Astrobiology

Atmospheric biosignatures, pebble accretion mechanisms in super-Earths and mini-Neptunes, and host star stellar flare activity impacts on exoplanetary atmospheres and habitable zone boundaries.

Deep Learning & AI in Cosmology

Applying convolutional neural networks, vision transformers, and generative adversarial networks to astronomical image denoising, gravitational lens detection, transient identification, and cosmological simulation analysis.

Impact & Output

Research Highlights & Alumni Pathways

From first observations atop AB-1 to international research contributions and doctoral programs.

JWST Observations

Submillimetre Galaxy Dynamics

Alumni work with international consortia examining the physical properties and dust attenuations of high-redshift submillimetre galaxies using integral field spectroscopy (IFU) and James Webb Space Telescope observations.

InPTA Collaboration

Pulsar Astrometry & InPTA

Active collaboration with Indian and international pulsar timing arrays to search for ultra-low frequency gravitational waves and investigate microsecond pulse profile variations from millisecond pulsars.

Doctoral & Master's Placements

IBARG alumni have successfully secured admission and prestigious fellowships at world-renowned institutions, including Columbia University, Adam Mickiewicz University (Poland), IUCAA Pune, and TIFR.

Applying to Join IBARG

We invite curious minds with passion for coding, data analysis, and observational physics.

Semester Intake Cycles

Recruitment for the IISER Bhopal Astronomy Research Group opens each semester: typically the second week of January for even semesters, and the second week of August for odd semesters.

Open to all IISER Bhopal students regardless of year or major. Prospective members are evaluated based on their statement of research interest, fundamental problem-solving aptitude, programming experience (Python, C++, or Julia), or enthusiasm for telescope hardware and observational nights.
