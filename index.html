Who We Are

About IBAC

The premier platform for amateur sky observers and aspiring astrophysicists at IISER Bhopal, bridging raw curiosity with rigorous observational science.

Stargazing & Telescope Nights

Regular open-roof observation sessions using our 14-inch Cassegrain and high-performance portable Dobsonian telescopes. We observe planets, lunar topography, nebulae, star clusters, and deep sky targets with the entire IISERB community.

Colloquia & Masterclasses

Hosting lectures by distinguished researchers, astrophysicists, and alumni. Past talks feature discussions on gravitational waves, pulsar timing, black hole relativistic jets, habitability, super-Earths, and active galactic nuclei.

Astrophotography & Imaging

Hands-on training in planetary, deep-sky, and landscape astrophotography. Members master camera stacking, image processing via software like SAOImage DS9, and precision exposure calibrations under Bhopal skies.

Scientific Research Training

Direct mentoring for students looking to pursue research careers in astrophysics, cosmology, and observational data reduction. Many of our alumni have progressed to top doctoral institutions across North America and Europe.

A Brief History of IBAC

Founded in 2015 by the then-secretary of the IISERB Science Council, Navin Sridhar (BS-MS 2013, alumnus). When Navin joined IISER Bhopal in August 2013, there was no student activity club for those who shared a passion for the cosmos. In 2014, upon becoming secretary of the Science Council, he availed sanctions from the institute to procure astronomical equipment and established the club, then named IISER Bhopal Astronomy Club (IBAC).

The club has flourished through seven generations of student leadership. Under the coordinatorship of Vineet Maurya, the construction of the permanent dome observatory atop the campus was completed and the student research wing (IBARG) was founded. On National Science Day (28 February 2019), IBAC launched the maiden edition of our flagship astronomy festival, "Hunting the Heavens".

Observational Equipment & Instruments:

14" Cassegrain Telescope

Housed in a campus observatory dome with German Equatorial Mount (GEM) for research & high-resolution astrophotography.

6" Dobsonian "Dobby"

Newtonian reflector on a smooth Dobsonian mount for portable campus stargazing camps and quick lunar/planetary viewing.

Skygazers' Binoculars

Two Olympus 10x50 and one Nikon Aculon binoculars for wide-field Milky Way observation and lunar eclipses.

Campus Activities & Astronomical Sessions

Events & Stargazing

From our annual flagship festival Hunting the Heavens (HTH) to live broadcasts of historic space missions and midnight telescope camps under dark skies.

Flagship Annual Fest

Hunting the Heavens (HTH 2026)

Inaugurated on National Science Day (28 February 2019), Hunting the Heavens is IBAC's signature inter-college astronomy festival featuring sky treasure hunts, astro-quizzes, astrophotography competitions, model-making, and dark-sky observation.

Annual Festival IISERB Campus
Large Screen Broadcast

Space Mission Livestreams

Live-screening milestone astrophysics and space exploration missions on large screens for the campus community — including the Chandrayaan-3 lunar landing, Aditya-L1 solar launch, and NASA rover touchdowns, drawing hundreds of students.

Chandrayaan-3 • Aditya-L1 LHC Big Screen
Stargazing Nights

Night-Sky Observation Camps

Hands-on sky tracking sessions deploying our 14" Cassegrain telescope in the dome and the 6" Dobsonian "Dobby" on the fields. Observers view Saturn's rings, Jupiter's Great Red Spot, Orion Nebula, and lunar crater chains.

Regular Night Camps Dome & Main Ground
Solar Observation

Solar Eclipse Sessions & SAC Fair

Equipped with certified Baader solar projection filters, IBAC organizes safe daytime viewing of solar eclipses, sunspots, and solar flares, as well as the Singularity Fest Solar Walk and SAC Orientation Fair for freshmen.
